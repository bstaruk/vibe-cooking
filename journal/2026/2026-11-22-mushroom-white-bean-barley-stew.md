# Mushroom, White Bean & Barley Stew: cook #1

**Verdict:** 5/5. A smash hit, made with one can of brown ale in the base. It was also the first cook in the Lancaster 5qt Dutch oven.

## What we did
- Full batch of recipe v2 (8 servings) in the Lancaster 5qt Dutch oven on the front-left zone. The pot had two inside coats of the house seasoning (Crisco, 500°F (260°C), 1 hr each) the day before, one more than the house standard.
- Baby bellas for the cremini (same mushroom).
- **Beer:** one 12 oz can of Wormtown Blizzard of '78, an English brown ale brewed with coffee (6% ABV), weighed at 355g. It went in after the tomato paste and simmered a few minutes. The stock dropped by the same weight, to 1025g. This became the recipe's *Beer* riff.
- **Timing:** target service moved from 5:00pm to 5:30pm. Knife work finished at 2:59pm, and the mushrooms went into the cold pot at 3:10pm.
- **Mushrooms:** steamed under the lid at 7/10 for ~10 min. After 20 min uncovered at 7/10, liquid was still pooled in the pot (3:40pm). A few ounces were ladled off and mixed into the stock, and the mushrooms browned at 7.5/10.
- **Vegetables:** started at 5/10, which only steamed them. Turned up to 6.5/10 to get color.
- **Finish:** about 100g (~100ml) more vegetable broth at the end for a looser stew and more flavor, on top of the mushroom liquid. No other thickening or loosening.
- Not weighed: the drained beans and the 2 bay leaves.
- Bread on the side: the American French bread Brian baked the day before.

## How it turned out
5/5, "a smash hit." Asked about flavor afterward, Brian called it savory, with the beer just right, so the stew doesn't need porcini to taste deep. Brian wanted it a little looser than the recipe made it, even with the extra mushroom liquid.

## Diagnosis
- **The mushroom liquid outlasted the timing.** Cremini are more than 90% water, so 900g gives off a lot of liquid. The 8¾" floor is a small surface for it to boil off from, and the 4⅛" walls hold steam over it. The recipe's 10–15 min was a guess for this pot, and 20 min at 7/10 wasn't enough. Ladling off the extra and adding it back with the stock keeps all the flavor and saves the wait.
- **5/10 was too low for 620g of cold vegetables.** A big wet load cools the pan, and if the zone can't drive off the water as fast as the vegetables release it, they stew instead of browning. 6.5/10 was enough. That's a fact about this zone and this pot, so it goes in the equipment notes too.
- **The barley keeps drinking.** Pearled barley soaks up liquid for the whole simmer and after, so the stew thickens as it sits. About 100g more stock at the end brought it to where Brian likes it, and using stock instead of water added flavor rather than diluting it.
