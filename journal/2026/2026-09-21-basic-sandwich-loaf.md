# Basic Sandwich Loaf: cook #1

**Verdict:** 5/5. The first bake of a recipe written the same day, followed to the letter, and it came out right.

## In Brian's words
> i made this exactly as you wrote.. like literally exactly.. and by 40 minutes baking it was 192-195f and i pulled it. it came out PERFECT. we are having grilled cheese tonight with it.

## What we did
- Full recipe, v1, no scaling and no swaps: 450g all-purpose flour, 140g half-and-half, 170g water from the 1L Bonavita kettle at 160°F (71°C), 70g salted butter, 9g coarse sea salt, 9g Fleischmann's active dry yeast from the freezer.
- Mixed in the 5qt All-Clad bowl and kneaded by hand. No mixer.
- Baked in the 1.5 lb Nordic Ware loaf pan at 350°F (175°C), regular bake.
- Pulled at **40 min**, the top of the recipe's 35–40 min range, with the Thermapen reading **192–195°F (89–91°C)** in the center.

## How it turned out
"PERFECT," in Brian's word. Headed straight into grilled cheese the same night. Not recorded for this cook: how long each rise took, which brand of all-purpose flour, and how the sides browned in the bare aluminum pan.

## Diagnosis
- **The pan sizing held.** Scaling King Arthur's 360g formula to 450g for the 9" × 5" pan gave a properly filled loaf, in line with their pan guidance.
- **The house substitutions worked on the first try:** half-and-half plus 10g extra water in place of milk, salted butter with 1g less added salt, and active dry yeast at 1.25× the instant amount, bloomed. None of them needs adjusting.
- **Freezer-stored active dry yeast is fine.** It bloomed and raised an enriched dough on a same-day schedule.
- **The loaf needed the full 40 min** to reach 192–195°F (89–91°C). That's one data point, but it suggests the Café oven isn't running hot at 350°F (175°C).
