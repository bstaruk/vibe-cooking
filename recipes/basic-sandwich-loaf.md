# Basic Sandwich Loaf

> A soft, lightly enriched white pan loaf, sized for the 1.5 lb Nordic Ware loaf pan and out of the oven about 3 hours after the flour hits the bowl.

**Serves** 8 · **Active** 30 min · **Total** 4 h · **Status** raw · v1

## Why this works

- **Sized to the pan.** King Arthur's loaf is written for an 8½" × 4½" pan and 360g flour. Their pan guidance puts a 9" × 5" pan at about 3¾–4 cups of flour, so everything here is scaled 1.25× to 450g. Too little dough in this pan bakes up squat with a flat top.
- **A full dose of active dry yeast (2%), bloomed in warm liquid,** gets the dough moving fast enough for a same-day loaf. The trade-off is flavor: less fermentation time means a milder loaf, and the half-and-half, butter, and sugar cover for that.
- **Half-and-half, butter, and sugar tenderize.** Half-and-half stands in for the source's milk because it's what the house keeps. It brings ~11g more fat than whole milk and a little less water, so the water goes up 10g. Fat coats gluten and sugar holds water, which gives the fine, soft crumb that slices thin and keeps for days.
- **Shiny bare aluminum browns gently,** so the sides and bottom will be paler than the top. The loaf comes out of the pan right away so the crust doesn't steam soft.

## Equipment

- **5qt All-Clad bowl**: mix and rise. The dough is kneaded by hand on the counter; one loaf is too small to be worth the mixer.
- **1.5 lb Nordic Ware loaf pan**: bake
- **Bench scraper**: keep the counter and hands clean while kneading
- **Bowl scraper**: get the shaggy dough out of the bowl
- **OXO scale**: everything over 50g
- **Timemore scale**: yeast, salt, sugar
- **1L Bonavita kettle**: heat the water to temperature
- **Thermapen**: liquid temperature and doneness
- **Café oven**: proof mode for the first rise, then bake
- Wire cooling rack; a nylon or silicone spatula if the loaf needs help releasing (no metal in this pan)

## Ingredients

- **170g** water (~¾ cup)
- **140g** half-and-half (~½ cup + 1 tbsp), cold from the fridge
- **30g** granulated sugar (~2½ tbsp)
- **9g** active dry yeast (~1 tbsp)
- **75g** salted butter (~5 tbsp), divided: 70g melted for the dough · 5g soft for the pan
- **450g** all-purpose flour (3¾ cups)
- **9g** coarse sea salt (~1½ tsp)

## Method

### Mix and knead

1. **Bloom the yeast.** Fill the 1L Bonavita kettle with at least 300g water and set it to **160°F (71°C)**. Set the 5qt All-Clad bowl on the OXO scale, pour in 170g of the heated water, and add 140g cold half-and-half. The mix should read **100–110°F (38–43°C)**; hot tap water alone lands too cool against fridge-cold half-and-half. If it's over 110°F (43°C), wait a minute or two before adding the yeast. Stir in 30g granulated sugar and 9g active dry yeast and let it stand **5–10 min**, until the surface is foamy and smells yeasty. No foam after 10 min means the yeast is dead; stop and replace it.
2. **Add the rest.** Pour in 70g melted salted butter, then add 450g all-purpose flour, and sprinkle 9g coarse sea salt on top of the flour. Stir with a stiff spatula or one hand for **1–2 min**, until a shaggy dough forms and no dry flour is left in the bowl.
3. **Knead.** Scrape the dough out with the bowl scraper onto a clean, unfloured counter and knead by hand for **8–10 min**: push it away with the heel of the hand, fold it back over itself, turn it a quarter turn, and repeat. It starts out sticky and gets less so as the gluten develops, so hold off on adding flour for the first 4 min; the bench scraper cleans the counter and hands. It's done when the dough is smooth, tacky but not sticking to a clean finger, springs back when poked, and no salt crystals feel gritty when a piece is rubbed between the fingers. If it's still smearing the counter after 5 min, knead in flour 10g at a time. If it's stiff and tearing, knead in water 10g at a time.

### First rise (60–75 min)

4. **Rise.** Round the dough into a ball, put it back in the 5qt All-Clad bowl (no need to wash or grease it), and cover the bowl. Set it in the Café oven on proof mode for **60–75 min**, until the dough is puffy and nearly doubled, and a floured finger pressed in ½" (1cm) leaves a dent that fills back slowly. At room temperature this takes closer to **1½–2 h**.
5. **Grease the pan.** While the dough rises, rub 5g soft salted butter over the inside of the 1.5 lb Nordic Ware loaf pan, corners and rim included. Bare aluminum isn't nonstick.

### Shape and second rise (45–60 min)

6. **Shape.** Take the dough out of the oven. Turn it onto a bare or very lightly floured counter and press it into a rectangle about 9" × 12" (23 × 30cm), pressing out the large bubbles. Starting from a short side, roll it up tightly into a 9" (23cm) log and pinch the seam shut. Set it seam side down in the greased loaf pan and cover loosely.
7. **Second rise.** Leave the pan on the counter for **45–60 min**, until the center of the dome stands about 1" (2.5cm) above the rim and a floured finger leaves a dent that springs back slowly. If the dent stays put, bake right away.
8. **Preheat.** About **20 min** into the second rise, check that the oven is empty, set a rack in the lower-middle position, and heat the Café oven to **350°F (175°C)** on regular bake, no convection.

### Bake

9. **Bake.** Bake **35–40 min**, until the top is deep golden brown and the Thermapen reads **190–195°F (88–91°C)** in the center of the loaf. If the top reaches deep golden before **25 min**, tent it loosely with foil.
10. **Turn out and cool.** Turn the loaf out of the pan onto the wire rack right away. If it sticks, loosen the sides with a nylon or silicone spatula. For a crisper bottom and sides, put the bare loaf back in the oven directly on the rack for **3–5 min**, until the sides feel firm and dry. Cool for **1 h** before slicing; the crumb is still setting and will gum up the knife if cut hot.

## Make ahead & storage

- **Room temperature:** in a bag or wrapped, cut side down, 3–4 days.
- **Freezer:** sliced and bagged, up to 3 months. Toast straight from frozen.
- **Pan care:** hand wash the loaf pan; the dishwasher discolors bare aluminum.

## Riffs

- *(untried)* **Mixer:** instead of kneading by hand (step 3), knead in the KitchenAid mixer with the spiral dough hook: speed 1 for about **1 min**, until no dry flour remains, then **speed 2** for **6–8 min**, until the dough is smooth and clears the sides of the bowl.
- *(untried)* **Bread flour:** swap the 450g all-purpose flour for 450g bread flour and raise the water from 170g to 180g. Chewier, taller loaf.
- *(untried)* **Part whole wheat:** use 340g all-purpose flour and 110g whole wheat flour, and raise the water from 170g to 185g.
- *(untried)* **Whole milk:** the source's version. Use 140g whole milk instead of the 140g half-and-half and drop the water from 170g to 160g.
- *(untried)* **Honey:** replace the 30g granulated sugar with 30g honey (~1½ tbsp).
- *(untried)* **Overnight:** after shaping (step 6), cover the pan and refrigerate **8–16 h**. Bake when the dome stands 1" (2.5cm) above the rim, letting it finish on the counter if it's short. More flavor for no extra work.
