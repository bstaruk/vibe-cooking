# Mushroom, White Bean & Barley Stew

> A hearty, savory vegan stew for the Lancaster Dutch oven. 900g of mushrooms are browned in the pot without batches, then simmered with miso, soy, and pearled barley. It contains no wine or tomato base, so it's gentle on new seasoning.

**Serves** 8 · **Active** 1 hr · **Total** 2 hr · **Status** raw · v2

## Why this works

- **Water first, oil after.** Crowded mushrooms with a splash of water collapse and release their own liquid. Once that liquid boils off, they brown in a dry pot with very little oil. That's how 900g fits on the Dutch oven's 8¾" floor without batches (ATK; Cooking Issues).
- **Savory without meat.** Browned mushroom fond, soy sauce, browned tomato paste, and miso are all rich in glutamates, and together they make the stew taste deep and meaty. ATK's bourguignons build on the same base and add dried porcini. Here porcini are a riff, because they're hard to find locally.
- **The barley thickens it, and the beans go in last.** Pearled barley simmers right in the stew, and its starch gives the stew body, so there's no flour or roux (ATK's barley soup works the same way). Canned beans are already cooked, so they only need to heat through.
- **Easy on young seasoning.** Lancaster says to build significant seasoning before cooking acidic foods. Lodge calls acidic recipes a poor choice for new cast iron, and ATK tasted metal in tomato sauce after 30 min in iron. So there's no wine and no tomato base. The only real acid is 16g tomato paste, browned and spread through ~3kg of stew. The stew comes out of the pot as soon as it's done.

## Equipment

- **Lancaster 5qt Dutch oven**: everything. The finished ~3kg fills it about two-thirds, the limit for a simmer.
- **5qt All-Clad bowl**: hold the browned mushrooms
- **8" Mac chef's knife**: vegetables. It's sturdier than the Takamura on hard carrots.
- **1L Bonavita kettle**: hot water to loosen the stew, if needed
- **OXO scale**: vegetables, stock, beans, barley
- **Timemore scale**: salt, thyme
- Flat wooden spatula (for scraping fond), ladle, fork

## Ingredients

- **900g** cremini mushrooms (2 lb), halved or quartered into ~1" (2.5cm) pieces
- **60g** water (¼ cup), for the mushrooms
- **14g** coarse sea salt (~2⅓ tsp), divided: 4g for the mushrooms · 4g for the vegetables · 6g with the stock, plus up to 4g more to taste
- **40g** olive oil (3 tbsp), divided: 27g for the mushrooms · 13g for the vegetables
- **~300g** yellow onion (1 large), ½" (1cm) dice
- **~200g** carrots (3 medium), ½" (1cm) dice
- **~120g** celery (3 ribs), ½" (1cm) dice
- **15g** garlic (~4 cloves), minced
- **16g** tomato paste (1 tbsp)
- **1g** dried thyme (1 tsp)
- **1380g** low-sodium vegetable stock (~5¾ cups; two 32 oz cartons cover it). Boxed is fine.
- **150g** pearled barley (¾ cup)
- **32g** soy sauce (2 tbsp)
- **34g** white or yellow miso (2 tbsp). Check that the label lists no dashi, bonito, or fish, so the stew stays vegetarian.
- **2** dried bay leaves
- **~480g** canned cannellini beans (2 cans, 15–15.5 oz each), drained and rinsed
- hot water from the kettle, as needed to loosen (60g at a time)
- black pepper, to taste (start ~1g)
- **10g** flat-leaf parsley, chopped (optional)

## Method

### Knife work

1. **Cut the vegetables.** Halve or quarter 900g cremini into ~1" (2.5cm) pieces, leaving small ones whole. Cut ~300g yellow onion, ~200g carrots, and ~120g celery into ½" (1cm) dice and keep them together. Mince 15g garlic and set it next to 16g tomato paste and 1g dried thyme.

### Brown the mushrooms

2. **Steam them down.** Put the 900g cremini, 60g water, and 4g coarse sea salt in the cold Lancaster 5qt Dutch oven on the front-left zone. It will be heaped. Cover and cook over medium-high heat **8–10 min**, stirring once halfway, until the mushrooms have collapsed to about half their volume and are sitting in their own liquid.
3. **Cook off the liquid.** Uncover and keep cooking over medium-high, stirring every couple of minutes, **10–15 min**, until the liquid is gone, the mushrooms sizzle, and a brown film of fond forms on the bottom.
4. **Brown.** Add 27g olive oil and stir to coat. Cook **5–8 min**, stirring only about once a minute, until the mushrooms are deeply browned in spots. If the fond gets darker than mahogany, turn the heat down. Move the mushrooms to the 5qt All-Clad bowl and leave the fond in the pot.

### Build the base

5. **Soften the vegetables.** Turn the heat to medium. Add 13g olive oil, the ~300g onion, ~200g carrots, and ~120g celery, and 4g coarse sea salt. Cook **8–10 min**, stirring and scraping up the fond as the vegetables release moisture, until the onion is soft and golden at the edges.
6. **Toast the paste.** Push the vegetables to one side. In the cleared spot, cook 15g garlic, 16g tomato paste, and 1g dried thyme, stirring, **1–2 min**, until the paste darkens to brick red and the garlic smells fragrant. Stir everything together.

### Simmer

7. **Add the liquids and barley.** Add 1380g low-sodium vegetable stock, 150g pearled barley, 32g soy sauce, 2 bay leaves, 6g coarse sea salt, and the browned mushrooms from the 5qt All-Clad bowl. Dissolve 34g miso in a ladleful of the hot stock with a fork, then stir it in. Scrape the bottom of the pot clean.
8. **Simmer the barley.** Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then lower the heat until you see small, steady bubbles at the edges. Set the lid ajar and simmer **45–50 min**, stirring and scraping the bottom every **10 min** so the barley doesn't stick, until the barley is tender with a slight chew.
9. **Add the beans.** Stir in ~480g drained cannellini beans and simmer uncovered **about 10 min**, until the beans are hot through and the stew coats a spoon. For a thicker stew, mash a ladleful of beans against the side of the pot. If it's too thick, stir in hot water from the kettle 60g at a time.
10. **Season.** Remove the 2 bay leaves. Add black pepper (start ~1g), taste, and stir in up to 4g more coarse sea salt. Stir in 10g chopped parsley, if using.
11. **Serve, then empty the pot.** Serving from the Dutch oven at the table is fine, but don't cool or store the stew in it. Move leftovers to shallow containers right after the meal, then wash and dry the pot.

## Make ahead & storage

- **Cool fast:** use shallow containers no more than 2" (5cm) deep, and get them in the fridge within **2 hours** of cooking. Don't put the whole hot pot in the fridge.
- **Fridge:** 3–4 days. Reheat to **165°F (74°C)**. If the stew has thickened in the fridge, loosen it with hot water from the kettle.
- **Freezer:** 2–3 months for best quality. Thaw in the fridge.
- **Not in the iron:** long contact with wet food rusts bare iron, and ATK advises taking acidic dishes out of the pan as soon as they're cooked.

## Riffs

- *(untried)* **Dried mushrooms, if you find them:** before step 1, soak 15g dried porcini (~½ oz) or dried shiitake (ATK's swap for porcini) in 240g boiling water (1 cup) from the 1L Bonavita kettle at **212°F (100°C)**. Soak in the 1.5qt All-Clad bowl **15–20 min**, until soft enough to chop. Chop them fine and add them in step 7. Pour in the soaking liquid (~180g), leaving the last spoonful and its grit in the bowl, and use 180g less stock (1200g). Chanterelles work too, but they're milder and fruitier.
- *(untried)* **Red wine, later:** after step 6, add 120g dry red wine and simmer **3–4 min**, until it's reduced by about half and no longer smells sharp. Save this until the Lancaster has plenty of seasoning built up, or make the stew in the 6qt All-Clad stockpot, where acid doesn't matter.
- *(untried)* **Greens:** in step 9, stir in 150g chopped Tuscan kale, stems removed, with the beans. Simmer until wilted and tender.
- *(untried)* **Dried beans:** cook 200g dried cannellini separately, and in step 9 use ~480g of the cooked, drained beans instead of the canned. ATK found that an acidic sauce slows beans as they cook, so finish cooking them before they go into the stew.
