# Cook profile: Brian

What Patina knows about how Brian cooks and eats. It gets updated during conversations and by `/debrief`. Dated entries show when we learned something.

_Last updated: 2026-09-13 (kitchen tour in progress)_

## Snapshot

- **Gear nerd:** loves specs, testing, and new tech. Open to gear suggestions when they're justified.
- **Cast iron devotee.** Collects vintage iron as well as modern. The Wapak No. 7 skillet is the favorite, used sparingly out of fear of warping it on induction.
- **Scale-first:** grams everywhere; °F with °C helpers. Weighs small amounts on a 0.1g scale and has a 0.01g scale for finer work.
- **Pizza is a serious pursuit:** a Gozney Arc XL, two perforated pizza pans, a Sicilian pan, pizza screens, and original south shore bar pizza pans. Makes poolish doughs.
- **Loves making bread,** especially with multi-day ferments. Prefers yeasted breads now; no longer keeps a sourdough starter.
- **Eats vegetarian more often than not.** Brian's wife is vegetarian. Brian isn't, but was vegetarian for 6 years and loves tofu.

## Household & rhythm

- **Cooks for:** Brian and his wife.
- **Weeknight time budget:** flexible.
- **Project cooking:**
  - **Loves:** projects whose payoff lasts. Examples: multi-day bread ferments, and batch days like making 200 pierogi to portion, vacuum-seal, and freeze.
  - **Doesn't love:** long hands-on cooks for a single meal. A 5-hour bolognese was "good but not 5 hours in the kitchen good."
